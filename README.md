
# HR Analytics Team Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

The HR Analytics Team Dashboard is a data analytics project designed to analyze workforce data and monitor key Human Resources (HR) metrics. It provides insights into employee headcount, attrition, gender diversity, department-wise distribution, job levels, employment types, and employee tenure.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall and active employee headcount.
- Monitor employee attrition and attrition rate.
- Track workforce movements, including onboarding and transfers.
- Analyze gender diversity across the workforce.
- Understand department-wise headcount distribution.
- Analyze employee job levels and employment types.
- Evaluate employee tenure and workforce stability.
- Support data-driven HR decision-making through interactive visualizations.

The dashboard tracks workforce movements from 2020 to 2025, including attrition, onboarding, and employee transfers.

**Key objectives:**
- Monitor historical workforce changes.
- Compare employee onboarding and exits.
- Identify workforce growth and reduction trends.

## 💡 Key Business Insights

1. The dashboard reports an overall headcount of 171 and an active headcount of 118.
2. The reported attrition rate is 27%, highlighting the importance of monitoring employee retention.
3. Male employees represent 62% of the displayed active workforce.
4. Operations accounts for the largest departmental share at 23%.
5. Permanent employees represent 89% of the displayed active workforce.
6. Professionals and trainees are the two largest job-level groups.
7. Historical workforce movement data can help HR identify changes in hiring and employee exits.

**Data validation:** Overall headcount (171), active headcount (118), and attrition (47) do not reconcile directly. KPI definitions and reporting periods should be verified before using the figures for formal reporting.
